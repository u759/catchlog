## Design Specificity Verdict
Content is specific (lakes, depth maps, Dex numbers, solunar); presentation is a generic kit: white rounded cards with icon + bold heading, uppercase micro-labels, three-stat tile rows on Logbook, Catch detail and Lake. Photos, species art and lakes are boxed at the same weight as settings cards.
